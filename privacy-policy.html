Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 9, 2026

San Jose Piano Lessons operates this website. This policy explains how information may be collected, used, and disclosed when you use the service.

Information collected

When using the service, you may be asked to provide personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify you, including email address, first and last name, phone number, cookies, and usage data.

Usage data may include your computer’s IP address, browser type and version, pages visited, time and date of visits, time spent on pages, device identifiers, and diagnostic data.

Cookies and tracking

The service may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to track activity and retain certain information. You can instruct your browser to refuse cookies or indicate when a cookie is being sent, though some portions of the service may not function as intended.

Use of data

Collected data may be used to provide and maintain the service, notify you about changes, provide customer support, improve the service, monitor usage, and detect, prevent, or address technical issues.

Transfer and disclosure

Your information may be transferred to and maintained on computers outside your state, province, country, or other governmental jurisdiction. San Jose Piano Lessons may disclose personal data when it believes in good faith that doing so is necessary to comply with a legal obligation, protect rights or property, prevent wrongdoing, protect safety, or protect against legal liability.

Service providers and external links

Third-party companies may facilitate the service or help analyze usage. They may access personal data only to perform those tasks and are obligated not to disclose or use it for another purpose. External websites are not operated by San Jose Piano Lessons; review their privacy policies separately.

Contact-form submissions are processed by FormSubmit and delivered to the studio by email. Appointment scheduling is provided by Acuity Scheduling. Information submitted to either service is also subject to that provider’s privacy practices.

Children’s privacy

The service does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under 18 without verification of parental consent. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact the studio.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated by posting a new version on this page. Questions can be directed to San Jose Piano Lessons by calling (408) 809-3626.
