Meet your instructors

Experienced teachers who care about how students learn.

Here at San Jose Piano Lessons, we value transparency in our methods and our instructors. The studio’s founding teacher is featured below.

Founding teacher

Mr. Malek

With over fourteen years of experience, Mr. Malek has taught piano to all ages and experience levels. His favorite thing about teaching is seeing how lessons help students become more disciplined and confident, no matter what age.

Several students have gone on to earn accreditation from international schools such as Royal College of Music and ABRSM. Though he specializes in classical music, Mr. Malek also teaches jazz, blues, and pop piano.

Mr. Malek comes from a musical family. As a pianist, he has performed with award-winning groups including the Flutesonic Chamber Orchestra and the LA Metropolitan Opera, and has performed on live television.
