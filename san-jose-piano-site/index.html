Learn your way

From your first note to advanced musicianship.

Lessons serve students from absolute beginners through experienced players. Students develop the language of music so they can read, listen, understand, improvise, practice effectively, and express themselves at the piano.

Instruction can focus on a specific goal: preparing for a recital, learning a meaningful piece, strengthening sight-reading and theory, moving into jazz improvisation, preparing for RCM or ABRSM exams, or becoming more comfortable performing in front of others.

Mr. Malek

With over fourteen years of experience, Mr. Malek has taught piano to all ages and experience levels. His favorite part of teaching is seeing how lessons help students become more disciplined and confident.

He specializes in classical music and also teaches jazz, blues, and pop. Students have prepared for international examinations including Royal College of Music and ABRSM. As a pianist, he has performed with the Flutesonic Chamber Orchestra, the LA Metropolitan Opera, and on live television.

Recitals & performance

Learning to play is also learning to perform.

Recitals bring together students of different ages, levels, backgrounds, and musical styles. Students are encouraged to present music that reflects who they are rather than fit into one recital theme.

Performance gives developing musicians a concrete goal, builds comfort playing in front of others, and lets newer students see what continued work can make possible.

Online lessons

The same teaching approach, wherever you are.

Live online lessons give students outside the South Bay, or students with demanding schedules, access to one-on-one instruction. Online students can work on repertoire, theory, reading, technique, improvisation, and performance preparation.

The studio also offers free tutorials for scales, notation, and songs, along with more in-depth video material.

Frequently asked questions

Before your first lesson.

What ages and experience levels do you teach?

All ages and experience levels, from absolute beginners to advanced musicians.

Do you offer a trial lesson?

Yes. The studio offers a free evaluation class so you can be paired with the right teacher and make sure the fit is right.

Do you prepare students for RCM or ABRSM exams?

Yes. Mention exam preparation when you schedule so the studio can pair you with the right teacher.

What is the cancellation policy?

The legacy policy requests 24 hours’ advance notice for cancellations while trying to remain accommodating.

Where can lessons take place?

At the student’s home, at an instructor’s home, or online. The business does not currently operate a dedicated school building.
