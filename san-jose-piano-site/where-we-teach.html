Flexible lesson locations

Piano lessons in San Jose and the surrounding Bay Area.

Choose the format that fits your student, instrument, schedule, and goals.

In-home lessons

Learn in a familiar place.

Teachers can travel to students in the South Bay and Santa Clara County. In-home lessons can be a comfortable option for children, busy families, and students who already have a piano or keyboard at home.

Availability depends on the student’s location and teacher schedule. Share your city and preferred times during the free evaluation.

Instructor-home lessons

A dedicated piano setting.

Students can also meet at an instructor’s home. This can work well for students who do not have their own piano or who prefer a dedicated lesson environment.

Teachers work with children and adults, beginners and advanced players, across classical, jazz, blues, pop, theory, sight-reading, and improvisation.

Service area

Serving the South Bay and Santa Clara County.

Teachers can travel to students, students can meet at an instructor’s home, and online lessons are available beyond the local service area.
